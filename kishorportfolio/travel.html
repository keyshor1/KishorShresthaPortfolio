My Travel Adventures

As someone who finds inspiration in exploring new places, I've been fortunate to visit some incredible destinations. Traveling allows me to disconnect from technology and reconnect with nature, culture, and myself. Each journey brings new perspectives and unforgettable memories that I carry with me in both my personal and professional life.



Sandakpur, Nepal

My journey to Sandakpur was an unforgettable experience. Located at 3,636 meters, it's the highest point in Ilam district. The sunrise view over the Himalayas was absolutely breathtaking, with panoramic views of four of the world's five highest peaks - Everest, Kanchenjunga, Lhotse, and Makalu. The trek through rhododendron forests and traditional villages gave me a deep appreciation for Nepal's natural beauty.

Mustang, Nepal

Exploring Mustang felt like stepping into a different world. The arid landscape, ancient caves, and Tibetan Buddhist culture created a unique atmosphere. I was fascinated by the walled city of Lo Manthang and the centuries-old monasteries. The challenging road trip through the Kali Gandaki gorge, the world's deepest gorge, added an extra layer of adventure to this remarkable journey.

Pokhara, Nepal

Pokhara has always been my favorite getaway destination. The serene Phewa Lake reflecting the Annapurna range creates a picture-perfect setting. I enjoyed boating on the lake, visiting the World Peace Pagoda, and exploring the vibrant Lakeside area. The city's relaxed atmosphere and stunning natural beauty make it the perfect place to unwind and reconnect with nature.

Ilam Tea Gardens

Visiting Ilam was a serene escape into Nepal's famous tea country. The rolling hills covered with lush tea plantations, combined with the cool climate and friendly locals, made it a perfect retreat. Walking through the tea gardens at sunrise and learning about tea processing gave me a deep appreciation for this beautiful region known as the "Queen of Hills."
